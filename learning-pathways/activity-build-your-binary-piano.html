Binary & Data • Lesson 1 • Activity 1

Your Mission

Build a Binary Piano that you can use to represent numbers using 0s and 1s.

You Will Need

  • Binary Piano template provided by your teacher
  • Scissors
  • Glue stick
  • Exercise book

Instructions

  1. Collect your Binary Piano template.
  2. Find the numbers printed on the template.
  3. Carefully cut along the marked cutting lines.
  4. Fold the tabs as shown by your teacher.
  5. Check that the tabs can move freely.
  6. Glue the Binary Piano into your exercise book. Do not glue the moving tabs down!
  7. Write your name beside your Binary Piano.

Try It Out

Find the place values:

  • 1
  • 2
  • 4
  • 8
  • 16

Notice how each place value is double the one before it.

Finished?

Show your teacher that your Binary Piano is assembled and the tabs work.

Completion check: I have built a Binary Piano with working tabs.
