Binary & Data • Lesson 1 • Activity 1

Your Mission

Build a Binary Piano that you can use to represent numbers using 0s and 1s.

You Will Need

  • Binary Piano template provided by your teacher
  • Scissors
  • Glue stick
  • Exercise book

Instructions

Follow the steps in order.

  1. 1📄CollectCollect your Binary Piano template.
  2. 2🔢FindFind the numbers printed on the template.
  3. 3✂️CutCarefully cut along the marked cutting lines.
  4. 4📐FoldFold the tabs as shown by your teacher.
  5. 5👆CheckCheck that the tabs can move freely.
  6. 6🧴GlueGlue the Binary Piano into your exercise book. ⚠️ Do not glue the moving tabs down!
  7. 7✏️Name itWrite your name beside your Binary Piano.

Try It Out

Find the place values:

  • 1
  • 2
  • 4
  • 8
  • 16

Notice how each place value is double the one before it.

Finished?

Show your teacher that your Binary Piano is assembled and the tabs work.

Completion check: I have built a Binary Piano with working tabs.
