Curriculum Mapping

Admin-only mapping of curriculum statements to the DTECH learning pathways, and of tracked activities to the statements they evidence. The Progression Pathway report uses this mapping to calculate pathway Coverage and to auto-set statements when students complete tracked activities.

Back to Admin Menu Open Progression Pathway

Mapping rules

  • Each curriculum statement has one coverage record per student; pathway Coverage is calculated from its linked statements.
  • A pathway is Not taught until a linked statement is covered, Partly taught once at least one is, and Taught only when every Primary and Supporting statement is Taught. Context dependent links count only once that statement is covered.
  • Only tracked activities with Auto-set Yes change coverage, and only from Not taught to Partly taught. Taught always needs teacher confirmation, and Progress descriptors are never auto-set.
  • Discipline defaults to DTECH. Mappings are year specific; Year 7 links are never applied to Year 8.
