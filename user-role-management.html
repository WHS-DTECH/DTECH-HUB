How It Works

  • Primary Role: Users automatically get their primary role from Google login and domain mapping rules.
  • Additional Roles: You can assign extra roles for special permissions without changing the base role.
  • Combined Access: Users can access all routes granted by any of their assigned roles.
  • Email Notification: You can hook up Gmail API to send role change emails later.
