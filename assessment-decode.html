DECODE – Read the Question

Learn how to break down exam questions by identifying the Action, Quantity, Context and Detail. Understand exactly what the question is asking so you can plan a complete, relevant answer without missing important requirements.

Four things to look for in every question

  1. 1Action

    What am I being asked to do?

  2. 2Quantity

    How many things are required?

  3. 3Context

    What must my answer refer to?

  4. 4Detail

    What specific information must I include?
