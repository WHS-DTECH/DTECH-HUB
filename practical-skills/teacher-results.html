Kit Content Builder Licence Kit Setup

Admin access required. Reading saved results does not change answers, completion or certificates, and sends no emails. Chocolate and submission Completed checkboxes save separately. In Student Review, verified teaching staff can explicitly approve document text checks after opening the student's document; this recalculates its activity tick and kit completion.

Class Kit Completion

See which active Licence Kits students have completed. Tick Chocolate when given; unchecked means No. Changes save automatically. Submitted drawings are listed under the table: open each link and tick Completed when the work is done. If Google verification has expired, allow the Google sign-in window to renew it; keep the same school account selected.

Course filters use recorded DTECH enrolment, not year level alone; MiddleDTECH shows MDTECH students. Choose a kit to filter students by its status or whether Chocolate has been given. Use Completed with Chocolate No to see who is still owed chocolate. Show selected kit only displays Student, the selected kit and its Chocolate column; turn it off to compare all kits.

Sign in with an admin account to load the class list.

Typing Kit - Weekly Results

Each week shows both saved typing periods (net WPM and accuracy). Period 1 signed and Period 2 signed record your exercise-book signatures only; they do not change typing completion or certificates. From Week 5, one saved test meets the weekly requirement; confirmed fully absent weeks are excused.

Current week uses the Typing Kit calendar and New Zealand date. Choose another week or All weeks to see other results and signatures.

Drawing links: Saved - sync needed means the link is saved but filing is not confirmed. Submitted - synced means DTECH-HUB successfully filed the drawing in the student's DTECH folder. Completed is the separate teacher review tick. Syncing does not change Google sharing permissions.

Enter feedback beside each drawing and select Save feedback. The student sees it in that drawing's activity. Feedback is separate from the Completed tick and is linked to the current drawing; save or clear it before refreshing the page.

Enter student emails from the email log to review their records.
